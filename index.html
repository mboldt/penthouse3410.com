Panoramic Views from the 34th Floor in Downtown St. Paul

Perched on the 34th floor of The Pointe, one of the tallest residential buildings in the Twin Cities, this two-bedroom corner penthouse delivers true panoramic views. To the north, the gold dome of the Minnesota State Capitol glows against the sky. To the west, the Minneapolis skyline rises above the tree line. To the south, the Mississippi River winds past downtown, with Pedro Park directly below.

Downtown St. Paul Location

The Pointe sits at the corner of Minnesota Street and 10th Street in downtown Saint Paul, directly across from Pedro Park and an Aldi grocery store. Within walking distance you'll find Xcel Energy Center, the Ordway Center for the Performing Arts, the Fitzgerald Theater, Rice Park, the Minnesota Children's Museum, the St. Paul Farmers Market in Lowertown, and restaurants along St. Peter Street.

The METRO Green Line 10th Street station is half a block away with direct light rail service to Minneapolis. Interstates 94 and 35E are minutes away, and Minneapolis–St. Paul International Airport is about 10 minutes by car.
